# 12 — Security, Privacy and Governance

**Status:** Principles established; legal review, retention schedule and detailed controls remain required.

## 1. Purpose

EcoWatch may hold sensitive reports, contributor contact details, location data, media and investigation records. Security and privacy must be designed into the system from the beginning.

## 2. Data minimisation

Collect only information needed for reporting, follow-up, verification or system operation. Anonymous reporting should remain possible where practical. Do not collect identity details simply because a form can.

## 3. Identity separation

Contributor identity and contact details should be stored separately from public incident information. Access should be restricted to authorised roles. Public-facing records should use a case or observation reference rather than exposing a contributor's identity.

## 4. Access control

Define roles and permissions before implementation. Candidate roles include:
- Anonymous contributor.
- Registered contributor.
- Reviewer.
- Investigator.
- Environmental specialist.
- Intervention manager.
- Organisation administrator.
- System administrator.

Role names do not automatically confer authority. Sensitive actions should require explicit permissions and be logged.

## 5. Upload security

- Restrict file types and file sizes.
- Do not trust a filename or browser-provided media type alone.
- Quarantine or scan uploads before wider access.
- Store files outside executable public web directories.
- Use controlled access links or application-mediated downloads.
- Prevent path traversal and unsafe file processing.
- Keep original files separate from generated previews.
- Monitor storage use and failed processing.

## 6. Personal and location privacy

Location information may identify a home, reporter or sensitive ecological site. Consider precision reduction for public maps. Do not publish exact reporter locations by default. Explain how submissions are used and who may access them.

## 7. Social media and external sources

Use only information and access methods permitted by the relevant platform and applicable law. Publicly visible information is not automatically free of copyright, privacy or contractual restrictions. Avoid collecting private messages or bypassing access controls.

## 8. Data governance

For each dataset, record:
- Owner or provider.
- Source and acquisition date.
- Licence or permitted use.
- Attribution requirements.
- Coverage and limitations.
- Retention and sharing restrictions.
- Responsible data steward.

Define who can approve publication, correct records, export data and respond to requests.

## 9. Audit and accountability

Record sensitive administrative actions, status changes, permission changes and access to restricted evidence where appropriate. Audit records should be protected from ordinary editing and reviewed periodically.

## 10. Retention and deletion

Create a retention schedule for:
- Anonymous reports.
- Registered contributor data.
- Uploaded evidence.
- Imported datasets.
- Case records.
- Audit logs.
- Backups.

Retention must balance investigation integrity, privacy, legal requirements and operational cost. The final policy requires local legal review and stakeholder approval.

## 11. Incident response

Before live operation, define:
- Who receives security reports.
- How compromised accounts are disabled.
- How exposed data is contained.
- How evidence and logs are preserved.
- How affected users or authorities are notified where required.
- How the incident is reviewed and corrective actions tracked.

## 12. Safety and non-retaliation

Do not expose reporter identity or encourage contributors to confront suspected polluters or enter dangerous areas. Public communication should avoid presenting unverified allegations as established facts. Sensitive cases require restricted handling and a safe referral pathway.

## 13. Legal review

Before launch, obtain qualified advice on applicable Ghanaian data protection, environmental, evidence, copyright, drone, cybersecurity and platform-access requirements. This document is not legal advice and does not certify compliance.
