# 16 — Security, Privacy and Governance

**Status:** Principles established; legal review and detailed policy required before live deployment.

## 1. Data minimisation

Collect only information needed for reporting, follow-up, verification or operation. Anonymous reporting should remain available where practical. Explain why contact details or location precision are requested.

## 2. Identity separation

Store contributor identity/contact information separately from ordinary observation and public case content. Restrict access to authorised roles. Public records should use references rather than contributor identity.

## 3. Access control

Candidate roles include contributor, reviewer, investigator, environmental specialist, intervention manager, organisation administrator and system administrator. Permissions must be explicit and enforced server-side. Sensitive actions may require separation of duties.

## 4. Upload security

- Validate file content and size.
- Quarantine or scan uploads using an appropriate process.
- Store files outside executable public directories.
- Use generated storage keys.
- Prevent path traversal and unsafe processing.
- Keep originals separate from previews.
- Use controlled access links or application-mediated downloads.
- Monitor storage and processing failures.

## 5. Location and personal privacy

Precise coordinates may identify homes, contributors or sensitive ecological sites. Restrict exact locations where necessary and generalise public geometry. Do not expose reporter locations by default.

## 6. External datasets and platforms

Public visibility does not imply unrestricted reuse. Respect licences, copyright, platform terms, privacy expectations and applicable law. Do not bypass access controls or depend on unauthorised scraping.

## 7. Governance register

For every dataset, record owner/provider, acquisition date, licence, attribution, coverage, method, limitations, retention and sharing restrictions. Assign a data steward and publication authority.

## 8. Auditability

Record sensitive access, role changes, review decisions, case status changes, evidence modifications, intervention approvals and verification conclusions. Protect audit records against ordinary editing.

## 9. Retention and deletion

Define schedules for contributor details, observations, evidence assets, imported data, case records, analysis outputs, audit logs and backups. Balance investigation integrity, privacy, legal duties and storage cost. Obtain qualified local legal advice.

## 10. Safety and non-retaliation

Do not encourage contributors to confront suspected polluters, trespass or enter unsafe areas. Protect vulnerable reporters. Public communication must not present unverified allegations as fact.

## 11. Legal review

Before live operation, obtain qualified advice on applicable Ghanaian data protection, environmental, evidence, copyright, drone, cybersecurity and platform-access requirements. This document is not legal advice and does not certify compliance.
