# 01 — Product Overview and Vision

**Status:** Product direction approved; pilot definition open.

## 1. Executive summary

EcoWatch is an environmental intelligence and response platform. It organises observations from communities, field teams, sensors, research, institutions and permitted remote-sensing sources; preserves their provenance; identifies potentially meaningful relationships; supports investigation and environmental analysis; helps responsible people prioritise work; records interventions; and tracks whether environmental conditions improve.

EcoWatch is not simply a reporting form, complaint map, sensor dashboard or chatbot. Its value is the traceable chain from an observation to an investigated case, a justified action and evidence about the outcome.

SignalWeave's report-correlation capabilities become a core subsystem called **Evidence Correlation and Incident Intelligence**.

## 2. Product problem

Environmental evidence is fragmented across people, organisations, formats, time periods and geographic systems. A resident's report, a water sample, a satellite observation, a sensor reading and an agency inspection may each describe part of a larger situation. They cannot safely be treated as equivalent, but they may be relevant to the same investigation.

Without a shared evidence model and workflow, teams may struggle to:
- Find related observations.
- Distinguish independent corroboration from repeated claims.
- Reconstruct changes over time.
- Understand upstream/downstream or land-water relationships.
- Identify evidence gaps and alternative explanations.
- Prioritise limited investigative resources.
- Track response activities and environmental outcomes.

## 3. Vision

A practical, evidence-led environmental intelligence system that helps communities, researchers and responsible organisations understand environmental change and support measurable recovery.

## 4. Mission

Make environmental observations more useful by preserving source and method information, connecting relevant evidence without erasing differences, analysing environmental change, supporting accountable decisions and tracking outcomes over time.

## 5. Core operating lifecycle

### Collect
Receive human and automated observations with source, location, time, method, permissions and quality information.

### Analyse
Correlate evidence, compare environmental conditions across time and space, identify patterns and examine possible relationships.

### Prioritise
Present transparent factors that help authorised people decide what needs attention. The system does not independently decide enforcement or public-safety action.

### Intervene
Record approved investigation, mitigation, referral, clean-up or restoration activities and their responsible parties.

### Verify
Compare appropriate indicators against baselines or reference conditions and document whether outcomes are supported, inconclusive or adverse.

## 6. Product principles

1. Evidence before automation.
2. Preserve original observations and files.
3. Distinguish claims, measurements, interpretations and model outputs.
4. Explain why evidence is linked.
5. Human review for consequential decisions.
6. Represent uncertainty rather than hiding it.
7. Protect contributors and sensitive locations.
8. Design for unreliable connectivity.
9. Scientific validity before model sophistication.
10. Activity completion is not proof of ecological recovery.

## 7. Product boundaries

EcoWatch supports environmental intelligence and workflow coordination. It does not replace regulators, laboratories, qualified environmental professionals, emergency services, courts or restoration practitioners. It does not automatically establish legal wrongdoing or environmental causation.

## 8. Initial context

The project is being developed with Ghanaian environmental challenges in mind. The long-term scope includes land, water and interconnected ecosystems. The pilot must still be geographically bounded and chosen using data availability, scientific relevance, stakeholder participation, safety and operational feasibility.
